Our Inspection Workflow

Every industrial NDT procedure follows a structured quality control protocol to guarantee valid results.

01

Preparation & Visual Calibration

The inspection surface is cleaned of rust, scaling, paint, and slag to ensure clean sensor contact. Inspectors carry out a primary visual check (VT) to identify obvious surface deformations.

02

NDT Scanning & Measurement

Our engineers perform high-end scan lines using calibrated equipment (e.g. Ultrasonic gauges, yoke magnets, penetrant dyes). All signals, ultrasonic waves, or dye indications are cross-verified.

03

Data Evaluation & Fault Isolation

Recorded metrics are mapped against the reference fabrication codes (ASME Sec VIII, API 650, etc.). Flaw locations are isolated, classified, and sizing indicators are marked on-site.

04

Certified Documentation & Reports

We generate detailed inspection worksheets complete with CAD mapping of defect locations, ultrasonic thickness grids, and clear recommendations signed off by certified NDT Level II/III inspectors.

Compliance Codes & Guidelines

Our inspections conform strictly to major international fabrication and safety associations.

Standard Body Relevant Codes Application Scope
ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) Section V (NDE), Section VIII (Boilers & Pressure Vessels), B31.1 / B31.3 Power boilers, pressure piping, process pipe welding audits.
API (American Petroleum Institute) API 570 (Piping), API 650 (Storage Tanks), API 653 (Tank Inspection) Refinery pipelines, bulk chemical terminals, atmospheric steel tanks.
ASTM (American Society for Testing & Materials) E165 (Penetrant), E709 (Magnetic Particle), E114 (Ultrasonic) Detailed testing process calibrations and material classification.
AWS (American Welding Society) D1.1 / D1.1M (Structural Welding Code - Steel) High-rise building joints, structural frameworks, steel civil infrastructures.
ISO (International Organization for Standardization) ISO 9712 (NDT Personnel Certification), ISO 17640 (Ultrasonic Weld Examination) Operational technician credentials and general testing methodologies.

Equipment Calibration & Reliability

To guarantee reliable inspection reports, Prime NDT International enforces strict calibration regimes. All transducers, thickness sensors, magnetic yokes, and ultraviolet lamps are certified periodically by accredited laboratories.

We adhere to international health, safety, and environment (HSE) codes. Radiographic source isotopes are handled in secure isolation under licensed radiation safety officers, keeping public risk levels at absolute zero.
