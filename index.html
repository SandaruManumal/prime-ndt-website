Our Core Inspections

Advanced non-destructive methods providing deep structural audits and subsurface fault evaluation.

Ultrasonic Flaw Detection (UT)

Internal volumetric testing of welds, joints, casting defects, and structural forgings utilizing high-frequency sound waves.

Magnetic Particle Inspection (MT)
