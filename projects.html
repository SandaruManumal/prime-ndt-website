Our Projects

At Prime NDT, we have successfully delivered inspection and testing services across a wide range of industries, including oil & gas, power generation, manufacturing, construction, and automotive. Our projects cover critical applications such as ultrasonic inspection of welds and forgings, magnetic particle and dye penetrant testing of structural components, and thickness measurements for tanks and vessels.

Each project reflects our dedication to safety, precision, and reliability, ensuring our clients' assets meet international standards and operate with confidence.

Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) Of Welding Joints Of The Thermic Oil Lines

Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to detect surface-breaking defects such as cracks, porosity, and lack of fusion. It was applied to the welding joints of thermic oil lines, which operate at high temperatures, to confirm weld quality and prevent leaks, ensuring safe and reliable operation of the system.

Fluorescent Penetrant Testing (FPT) Of Blades Of The Gas Turbine

Fluorescent Penetrant Testing (FPT) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to detect fine surface-breaking defects such as cracks and porosity. It was carried out on gas turbine blades under ultraviolet light, where the fluorescent dye clearly reveals even very small flaws. This helps ensure the blades are safe and reliable for operation under high temperature and stress.

Contact Us

Ultrasonic Testing Of Welded Joints Of Roll Trailer Gooseneck

Ultrasonic Testing (UT) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to detect internal defects in welded joints. It was applied to the welded joints of a roll trailer gooseneck, a critical load-bearing section, to identify cracks, lack of fusion, and other discontinuities. This ensures the structural integrity and safe operation of the trailer under heavy loads.

Contact Us

Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT) of Boilers And Pressure Vessel

Magnetic particle testing (MPT) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to detect surface and near-surface defects in ferromagnetic materials. It is commonly applied to boilers, pressure vessels and gas storage tanks to ensure structural integrity and safety.

Contact Us

Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UTG) Testing of Storage Tanks

Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UTG) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to measure the remaining wall thickness of storage tanks. It plays a crucial role in assessing tank integrity, detecting corrosion, and preventing leaks or failures. By utilizing high-frequency sound waves, UTG provides accurate thickness measurements without requiring access to both sides of the tank wall.

Contact Us

Ultrasonic Testing (UT) Of Clamp Of The Mold

Ultrasonic Testing (UT) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) method used to detect internal flaws in metal components. It was carried out on the clamp of a mold to identify cracks, voids, and other discontinuities that could weaken the part. This helps ensure the clamp’s strength and reliability during mold operations.
