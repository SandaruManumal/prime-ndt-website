Our Technical Capabilities

Ensuring complete safety compliance through high-end volumetric, surface, and visual inspection methods.

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

High-frequency sound waves are directed into the material to identify internal volumetric defects (e.g. slag inclusions, cracking, porosity) in welds, castings, and heavy forged machinery elements.

Request Quote ➔

Radiographic Testing (RT)

Utilizing X-ray or Gamma-ray sources to capture internal shadow images of pipelines and pressurized vessels, registering internal inconsistencies on photographic film or digital sensors.

Request Quote ➔

Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)

Using electromagnetic yokes to induce magnetic flux lines inside ferromagnetic steel. Magnetic particles are applied to expose surface cracks, seams, and near-surface structural discontinuities.

Request Quote ➔

Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)

Applying high-visibility liquid dye to non-porous welds, allowing capillary action to draw dye into open fractures. A developer is then used to draw out the dye, highlighting flaws visually.

Request Quote ➔

Visual Inspection (VT)

Essential primary testing method featuring detailed visual analysis of welds, structural surfaces, mechanical corrosion profiles, and misalignment checks carried out by experienced inspectors.

Request Quote ➔

Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UTG)

Measuring the thickness of metal walls in pipelines, storage silos, and vessel shells. It calculates corrosion rates and determines the active life cycle of components without destructive cuts.
