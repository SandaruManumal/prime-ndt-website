Our Services

Ensuring complete safety compliance through high-end volumetric and surface inspection methods.

Ultrasonic Scanning (UT)

Ultrasonic testing for forgings, castings, and welded components to detect internal defects and ensure structural integrity.

Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)

Surface and subsurface inspection of ferromagnetic materials.

Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging

Accurate wall thickness measurement of pipelines, tanks, and vessels to monitor corrosion and determine remaining service life.

Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)

Color contrast and ultraviolet examination for locating surface-breaking defects.
