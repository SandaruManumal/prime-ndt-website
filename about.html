About Our Company

Quality, Integrity & Trust

Your Trusted Partner In Industrial NDT Auditing

PRIME NDT INTERNATIONAL is a premier commercial testing organization, providing high-end industrial testing solutions and engineering inspections. We cater to major operations, including petroleum refinery facilities, chemical plants, pipelines, and civil fabrications.

Backed by international certifications (including ASNT/ISO guidelines), our operations deliver absolute accuracy. We ensure that critical components satisfy code thresholds, helping operators identify weld flaws, corrosion, cracking, or wall degradation before they lead to structural failure.
